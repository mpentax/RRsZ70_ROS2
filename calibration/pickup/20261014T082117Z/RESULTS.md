## What failed / what to improve

1. **Gripper observability:** close objects fill and blur the pincher lens, and neither view reliably resolves opposed finger contact. Calibrate camera-to-fingertip geometry and minimum focus distance; define a grasp reference in pixels rather than assuming image centre. A tactile/retention proxy may be useful, but cannot silently replace this brief's visual criterion.
2. **Release command:** `app_open_gripper {}` produced unknown/no-reply outcomes locally and a 30 s cloud timeout; images did not demonstrate release. Resolve the actual firmware open/put-down semantics offline before relying on it again.
3. **Unexplained base heading change:** frame 32 changed strongly after the second `app_put_down {flag:0}` and rapid stow/deploy. Association is not proof of causation. Audit that vendor routine and concurrent controller activity before another live trial; do not assume put-down is chassis-stationary. No vendor autonomous pickup was invoked in this run.
4. **Unreliable held odometry:** `/odom` kept publishing timestamps and zero twist while its pose was held after a rejection. Fresh timestamps/zero twist are not fresh motion evidence. Expose acceptance/freshness beside pose and do not use held VO as proof of stopped chassis.
5. **Arm execution versus ACK:** post-grip jog ACKs did not produce visible motion. Readiness, legal model motion, physical motion and settled state need distinct evidence. The rapid last stow/deploy sequence was not proved complete and was not blindly replayed.
6. **Trial accounting:** two attempts were made, with no strict success. The requested normal terminal condition (SUCCESS or three failed grasps) was not reached because a genuine operating-rule stop intervened. No fabricated third attempt, no success from ACKs, and no claim of independently confirmed physical stow.
